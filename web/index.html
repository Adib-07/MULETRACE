Catch mule-account networks as the money moves

Stolen money is split across many accounts and passed on within minutes, so each payment looks normal on its own. MuleTrace scores every payment against the last hour of money flowing around it, explains why, and leaves the decision to an investigator.

          How a payment flows

          1 · Payment arrivesValidated: time order, amount, accounts
          →
          2 · Flow memoryLast 60 minutes of money in/out per account
          →
          3 · 20 signalsFan-in, forwarding, loops, bursts + payment facts
          →
          4 · RecommendationPASS / FLAG / HOLD + reasons (XGBoost, CPU)
          →
          5 · Human decidesConfirm / escalate / dismiss · audit log
          HOLD and analyst actions are simulated: no real payment is held, released or reported.
